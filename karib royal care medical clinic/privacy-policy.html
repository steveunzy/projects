Privacy Policy

Your Privacy is Important to Us

Introduction

KARIB Royal Care Medical Clinic ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how we collect, use, and protect your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices concerning personal data and your rights in relation to that data.

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information necessary to provide healthcare services and maintain quality patient care. This includes:

  • Personal Identification Data: Name, date of birth, gender, national ID, contact information
  • Medical Information: Medical history, diagnoses, medications, allergies, test results, and treatment records
  • Contact Information: Email address, phone number, physical address
  • Insurance Information: Insurance policy details and coverage information
  • Billing Information: Payment details and transaction history
  • Website Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and usage analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Providing medical diagnosis, treatment, and healthcare services
  • Maintaining accurate patient medical records
  • Processing appointments and scheduling
  • Processing payments and managing billing
  • Communicating with you regarding your healthcare and appointments
  • Conducting medical research (with appropriate consent)
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Improving our services and website functionality
  • Analyzing trends and usage patterns for quality improvement

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our security measures include:

  • Encrypted data transmission using SSL/TLS protocols
  • Secure storage of patient records with access controls
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Staff training on data protection practices
  • Restricted access to sensitive information

However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties without your consent, except as required by law. Your information may be shared with:

  • Healthcare providers involved in your treatment (with your consent)
  • Insurance companies for coverage verification and claims processing
  • Government health authorities for disease surveillance and public health purposes
  • Legal authorities when required by law
  • Our service providers who assist in clinic operations (e.g., IT support, billing)

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal and medical information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for use of your information
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Request a copy of your medical records
  • File a complaint with relevant data protection authorities

6. Retention of Information

We retain your personal and medical information as required by law and for the duration necessary to provide healthcare services. Medical records are typically retained for:

  • A minimum of 5-7 years following your last visit
  • Longer periods if required for legal, regulatory, or medical purposes
  • Information related to minors is retained until the age of majority plus applicable retention periods

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are primarily designed for adults and minors seeking healthcare. For minors, we obtain medical information and consent from parents or legal guardians. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without parental consent. A minor's parent or guardian may contact us to review, update, or delete information.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your user experience. These technologies help us:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze website usage and improve functionality
  • Provide personalized content

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any websites before providing personal information.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, and other factors. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy on our website with an updated "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
